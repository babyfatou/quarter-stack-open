How to Open a Bank Account

This guide explains the basic steps for opening a bank account.

Before You Go

Choose a bank and find out what you need to open an account.

Choose a Bank

Compare banks and choose one that offers the services you need.

Check the Requirements

Ask the bank which documents you need and whether you must make an initial deposit.

What to Bring

Bring a valid form of identification and any other documents requested by the bank.

At the Bank

Ask a staff member for an application form and complete it carefully.

After Opening the Account

Follow the bank's instructions and keep your account information safe.
