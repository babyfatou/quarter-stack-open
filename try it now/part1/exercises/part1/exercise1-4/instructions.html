Generator Safety Instructions

These instructions are for anyone using a generator, especially when tired or in a hurry.

What you need

  • A generator
  • Fuel recommended by the manufacturer
  • Appropriate PPE
  • The generator's user manual

Steps

  1. Never run a generator indoors or in an enclosed space. Carbon monoxide can kill.
  2. Place the generator outdoors, far from doors, windows and air vents, following the manufacturer's instructions.
  3. Check the generator for damage before starting it.
  4. Start the generator according to the user manual.
  5. Before refuelling, turn it off and let it cool completely.
  6. Use the generator outdoors, not inside a house or garage.

If something goes wrong

If you smell fuel, see a fuel leak or notice damage, turn off the generator if it is safe to do so and keep away. If anyone feels dizzy or confused, move to fresh air immediately if safe and seek emergency medical help.
